## Logo Guidelines

### Primary Logo
The Webifyd Technologies logo consists of:
- A distinctive "W" symbol with geometric angular design
- Company name "Webifyd" in bold, modern typography
- Subtitle "TECHNOLOGIES" in lighter weight
- Tagline "Digital Solutions You Can Trust"

### Logo Variations
1. **Full Logo**: Complete logo with tagline (primary use)
2. **Logo without Tagline**: Company name and symbol only
3. **Symbol Only**: "W" mark for favicon, social media, etc.
4. **Horizontal Layout**: For wide applications
5. **Stacked Layout**: For square or vertical spaces

### Clear Space
Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "W" symbol on all sides.

### Minimum Size
- Digital: 120px width minimum
- Print: 1 inch width minimum

## Typography

### Primary Typeface: Modern Sans-Serif
**Recommended**: Inter, Roboto, or similar clean sans-serif
- **Headings**: Bold (700) and Semi-Bold (600)
- **Body Text**: Regular (400) and Medium (500)
- **Captions**: Light (300)

### Logo Typography
The logo uses a custom bold sans-serif typeface that conveys:
- Modernity and innovation
- Strength and reliability
- Professional expertise

### Typography Hierarchy
**H1 - Main Headlines**
- Font Weight: Bold (700)
- Size: 32-48px digital, 24-36pt print
- Color: Deep Navy Blue

**H2 - Section Headers**
- Font Weight: Semi-Bold (600)
- Size: 24-32px digital, 18-24pt print
- Color: Deep Navy Blue

**H3 - Subsections**
- Font Weight: Medium (500)
- Size: 18-24px digital, 14-18pt print
- Color: Webifyd Blue

**Body Text**
- Font Weight: Regular (400)
- Size: 16px digital, 12pt print
- Color: Dark Gray
- Line Height: 1.5

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice Characteristics
**Professional**: Expert knowledge communicated clearly
**Confident**: Assured in capabilities and solutions
**Approachable**: Friendly and accessible communication
**Solution-Oriented**: Focus on solving problems
**Innovative**: Forward-thinking and progressive

### Tone Guidelines
**When discussing technology**: Expert but not overly technical
**When addressing challenges**: Confident and solution-focused
**When building relationships**: Warm and trustworthy
**When presenting solutions**: Clear and benefit-focused

### Language Style
- Use active voice
- Be concise and clear
- Avoid jargon when possible
- Focus on benefits, not just features
- Include calls-to-action

## Do's and Don'ts

### Logo Do's
✅ Use the logo on clean, uncluttered backgrounds
✅ Maintain proper clear space
✅ Use approved color variations
✅ Scale proportionally
✅ Use high-resolution files

### Logo Don'ts
❌ Don't stretch or distort the logo
❌ Don't use on busy or conflicting backgrounds
❌ Don't change colors outside approved palette
❌ Don't separate logo elements
❌ Don't use low-resolution files
❌ Don't add effects (shadows, gradients, etc.)

### Color Do's
✅ Use colors consistently across all materials
✅ Maintain proper contrast ratios for accessibility
✅ Use neutral backgrounds for maximum impact
✅ Test colors across different media

### Color Don'ts
❌ Don't use colors outside the approved palette
❌ Don't use low-contrast combinations
❌ Don't overwhelm with too many colors
❌ Don't ignore accessibility guidelines

### Typography Do's
✅ Maintain consistent hierarchy
✅ Use approved font weights
✅ Ensure readability across all sizes
✅ Align text properly

### Typography Don'ts
❌ Don't use more than 3 font weights in one design
❌ Don't use fonts outside the approved family
❌ Don't sacrifice readability for style
❌ Don't ignore spacing and alignment
